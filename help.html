Understanding QLCS line analysis

A one-page conceptual guide to the three color modes, line-normal/parallel shear, and the 40-km inflow reference.

What is plotted on the traced line?

The line itself changes color. The gradient follows the leading-edge trace, highlighting differences between segments without turning them into yes/no categories.

QTor/weighted line gradient Tortuosity gradient Inflow reference Line-normal Motion

The gold ribbon extends 40 km ahead of the line, where inflow environmental sampling would conceptually occur. In this version it is a geographic reference—not a demonstrated RAP sampling corridor, warning polygon or tornado swath.

Red means a lower value, not zero risk. The shear profile is site-representative, not a time-varying forecast at every line segment.
